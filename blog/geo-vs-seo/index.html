Which channels actually improve your product's visibility in AI answers?

内容在什么渠道发力,才能真正优化产品在 AI 的可见度?

Mia Zheng August 12, 2026 2026年8月12日 3 min read 阅读约 3 分钟 GEO

Hello guys — we're researching GEO right now. It's basically the upgraded version of SEO.

Our ICP has gradually shifted from searching on Google to asking ChatGPT and Perplexity. We're no longer putting most of our budget into Google Ads.

✦
The shift

Ranking #1 on Google is no longer the goal. The goal is to get your product cited more often inside ChatGPT and other model answers. You need more AI to quote you.

Our ICP moved from Google to ChatGPT

This is the part that changes everything downstream. If your buyers aren't opening a search results page anymore, then every dollar you spend optimizing for that page is spent on a surface they don't look at.

So our goal now is: how do we get our product to appear more often inside ChatGPT answers, or inside other language models' answers?

The goal: from ranking to being cited

Ranking first on Google is no longer what matters. What matters is that more AI cites you.

That's a different job. Ranking is about matching a query. Being cited is about being in the sources the model can reach.

Why Reddit is the channel to build

On the question of which channels to target, I did some research today and want to share it with you: focus on building your presence on Reddit.

Why do I say that? Here we need to look at it from a more technical, or more commercial, angle. Try it yourself — every time you ask AI something, look at which platforms and which pages it searched.

i
Here's the answer

Reddit signed official data licensing agreements with both Google and OpenAI (the 2024 deals). So Reddit content gets crawled into AI answers on a priority, stable basis. This isn't a soft metric like "community trust" — it's an indexing privilege guaranteed by contract.

Why X isn't the same bet

Since Musk took over X (Twitter), external access and crawlers have been tightened. Google's crawlers have a hard time reliably crawling content on X, so you almost never see X as a source inside Google AI Overview.

Perplexity can occasionally pull some X content, but it's far less stable than Reddit.

Reddit
Contractual index access

Licensed to Google and OpenAI. Crawled on a priority, stable basis. Shows up consistently as a cited source.

X / Twitter
Restricted crawling

Tightened external access post-acquisition. Rarely appears in Google AI Overview. Perplexity pulls it occasionally, unreliably.
