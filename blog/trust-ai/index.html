Can marketing actually trust the results AI brings?

Marketing真的能相信AI带来的效果吗

Mia Zheng August 26, 2026 2026年8月26日 4 min read 阅读约 4 分钟 Growth

If you're fantasizing about building an agent team so you can sit back and watch users and revenue grow on their own, you've got it very wrong. I know there are a lot of people posting right now about how much money AI made them, or how many followers AI got them for them — either they're lying, or they spent an enormous amount of time training that AI to do the work.

My take: AI can only speed up how fast you find the path that works. The call is still yours.

Last week I sat in on an online session from Zapier and Clay about how they grow through social media. In the Q&A, someone asked how they use AI these days. Almost every one of them said they don't really depend on AI. They use it to dramatically speed things up, or to visualize results really well. But the core decisions aren't made by AI. (The session was long and packed with substance — I'm still writing up my notes.)

For senior marketing, you have to keep your feel for it

You can't let your AI understand your prospects better than you do — if it does, the role should go to the AI. Your customers' pain and needs — you have to be able to say them out loud, not have to look at some long report AI hands you every time. You need to sit inside the customer quotes AI feeds you and feel what they're feeling.

Feel is really just empathy. That's something only humans have.

An example: I'll have Codex browse Reddit for me and pull together what my prospects are saying is painful, and Codex hands it back to me organized. But what I actually reply with, I have to think through myself. That's how you actually feel the pain your customer is in — and then you can have a real conversation with them.

Customers aren't going to pay because of the AI video or image you made

This is something I figured out recently. I'm sure when you scroll short videos, you've come across cute little cats and dogs dancing — very short clips that somehow have tens of thousands of likes. You probably liked them too, and were amazed AI can make videos like that now. But look, the audience for that kind of video is not your customer.

You all know a B2B customer's decision cycle is long — they usually have to read your product materials, compare you against competitors, run a trial, and discuss internally before they commit. You might generate a product intro demo or an animation with AI, but the customer is absolutely not going to buy your product because of that video. Making people laugh with an AI video is easy. Making a B-end customer pay for something? Not likely. You can't expect someone to see a fake thing and then hand you real money for it. There's still a gap between what AI delivers and what a product actually delivers.

如果你妄想建立一个agent 团队，你就可以坐等用户规模和收入自增长，那就大错特错了。我知道现在有很多人都在发帖说自己的ai给自己赚了多少钱，或者帮他们做了多少粉丝，首先要么他们在说谎话， 或者他们花了非常多的时间在调教他们的ai在帮他们干活。

我的看法是，AI只是能加速你找到成功的路，但是决定权还是在你。

我上周听了Zapier和Clay的线上分享会， 讨论怎么在通过社交媒体做增长的，在Q&A环节有人问，你们现在是怎么用ai的。 几乎他们每一个人给到的答复都是不会怎么依赖于AI的。虽然他们利用ai大幅的提高了效率，或者说将结果很好的可视化了。but 核心决策不会是ai来完成的。（他们的分享很长很干货，我正在整理当中）

对于Senior Marketing来说，你需要保持触感

你不能让你的ai比你更懂你的潜在客户，不然这个职位你给ai做得了。你的客户的痛点和诉求是什么， 你需要说的出来，而不是每次都需要看ai给你发的冗长的报告。你需要在ai发给你的客户原声里面感受潜在客户的痛苦， 
触感其实就是共情，这是人才有的能力。
我举个例子，我会让codex帮我去操作浏览reddit看看我的潜在客户都在说什么痛点，codex整理好给我，但是最终回复的内容需要你自己去想，这样你就可以真切的感受到客户的痛点是什么，然后和客户对话。

客户不会因为你做的AI 视频/图片买单

这个是我前段时间突然想明白的。我相信你们刷短视频的时候都会刷到过很可爱的小猫小狗在跳舞，很短的视频居然有上万的点赞。这个时候你应该也会点赞，同时惊叹ai的视频居然能做到这个程度。 But请注意这种视频面向的人群和你的客户不一样。

你们都知道B2B的客户决策周期很长，他们一般要经过看过你的产品资料、不同竞品对比、试用以及内部商讨才能确定。你可能用ai生成一个产品介绍的demo或者动画效果， 但是客户绝对不会因为这个视频而为你的产品买单的。用AI视频让大家开怀一笑是容易的，但是让B端客户付费是不太可能的。你不能指望他看了一个假的东西然后让他付出真金白银。AI传递->产品交付还是有段距离的。
